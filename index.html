About关于我

A short introduction. 一段简短的自我介绍。

I am a Ph.D. student at the AI for Science Center and the School of Life Sciences, Northeast Normal University. My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, bioinformatics, and life science, with a particular focus on computational immunology and biological foundation models. 我是东北师范大学人工智能科学中心与生命科学学院的博士研究生，研究方向位于人工智能、生物信息学与生命科学的交叉地带，聚焦于计算免疫学与生物基础模型。

I work on how antigen-specific T-cell receptors recognize peptide–MHC complexes, on protein and single-cell foundation models together with parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and on the structural dynamics of interacting proteins. What keeps me going is the moment a model stops being a score and starts being a picture of the biology — a binding interface, a conformational ensemble, a cell state. 我的工作包括：抗原特异性 T 细胞受体如何识别肽段–MHC 复合物；蛋白质语言模型与单细胞大模型及其参数高效微调；相互作用蛋白的结构动力学。真正让我着迷的是这样一个瞬间：模型不再只是一个分数，而是开始呈现生物学的图像——一个结合界面、一组构象集合、一种细胞状态。
